# PriceWatch - Chrome Web Store Listing

## Extension Name
PriceWatch - Price Drop Tracker

## Short Description (132 chars max)
Track product prices and get notified when they drop. Save any product, set a target price, and never miss a deal.

## Detailed Description

**PriceWatch** lets you track product prices across any online store and notifies you when prices drop.

### How it works:
1. Visit any product page and click the PriceWatch icon
2. The extension detects the product name, image, and price automatically
3. Optionally set a target price — or get notified on any price drop
4. The extension checks prices in the background every few hours
5. Get a desktop notification when your tracked item drops in price

### Features:
- **Automatic price detection** from popular sites (Amazon, eBay) and any site using schema.org markup
- **Price history sparkline** — see price trends at a glance
- **Smart notifications** — deduped, with 24-hour cooldown so you're not spammed
- **Full control per item** — pause tracking, set target price, or delete
- **Sort your list** by most recent change, biggest drop, name, or date added
- **Export/Import** your tracked items as JSON
- **Notification mute** — silence for 1 hour, 8 hours, or 1 week
- **Dark mode** support

### Privacy:
- **No account required** — everything stays on your device
- **No server** — all data stored locally in your browser
- **No tracking** — we don't collect any data about you or the sites you visit
- **No cross-device sync** — your tracked items stay on this browser

### Permissions explained:
- **Storage**: Save your tracked items and settings
- **Alarms**: Check prices in the background without draining battery
- **Notifications**: Alert you when prices drop
- **Active Tab**: Read prices only from pages you explicitly click the icon on

---

## Privacy Policy

PriceWatch is a local-only extension. All data is stored on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API. No data is sent to any external server. We do not collect, transmit, or store any information about you, your browsing history, or the products you track.

When checking prices in the background, the extension fetches product pages directly from the retailer. These requests appear as normal browser requests. No identifying information is included.
